The site credits the studio with a single small line inside the existing dark footer, under the copyright line: "Design e Desenvolvimento por Black Dog Studio", linked to https://www.blackdogbr.com/ (opens in a new tab).

The user rejected a full-width cream band as "um footer enorme" — the credit is only a complement to the footer that already exists, so keep it at copyright-line scale and inside the same dark surface. Never grow it back into a separate section or a bright block.
